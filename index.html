What we care about

Our areas of focus

We believe meaningful progress starts with meeting essential needs and building inclusive communities.

NOURISH

Food security

Promoting access to nutritious food and community-based support for children and families.

EDUCATE

Primary education

Helping create opportunities for children to learn, grow and reach their potential.

INCLUDE

Autism awareness

Encouraging understanding, acceptance, resources and support for autistic children and their families.

EMPOWER

Down syndrome inclusion

Advocating for dignity, opportunity and belonging for people with Down syndrome.

SUPPORT

Healthcare awareness

Sharing knowledge and connecting communities with information about supportive services.

CONNECT

Family and community

Building welcoming spaces for families, volunteers and community partners.

Who we are

Rooted in compassion. Driven by community.

Chozhan Foundation is inspired by the belief that every child deserves access to nutrition, education and acceptance, regardless of ability or background.

Our vision brings together food security, foundational learning, disability inclusion and support for families, guided by the Tamil principle of universal kinship.
